Flow Blueprints

The 5 Core Revenue Flows

These 5 automated flows generate over 80% of all automated retention revenue when properly configured with dynamic branching logic.

1. Welcome Series (Non-Buyers)
Email 1 (Instant): Code delivery + Hero product spotlight.
SMS 1 (+5 Mins): Short greeting & auto-applied cart link.
Email 2 (+24 Hours): Founder vision & quality differentiator.
Email 3 (+48 Hours): 3–5 reviews handling top objections.
2. Cart & Checkout Recovery
Email 1 (+30 Mins): Helpful support angle ("Did something happen?").
SMS 1 (+60 Mins): Urgency reserve link before items release.
Email 2 (+12 Hours): Friction-removal: Free returns & warranties.
Email 3 (+24 Hours): Limited-time incentive (gift / free shipping).
🌿 DYNAMIC AOV SPLIT ENGINE TRIGGER: CART SIZE THRESHOLD
Branch A: Low-AOV Cart (< $75) Standard urgency timer + Free shipping milestone threshold.
Branch B: VIP High-AOV ($75+) Direct concierge support + Free Gift with Purchase incentive.
3. Browse Abandonment
Trigger: Viewed item 2+ times in 48h without cart add.
Email 1 (+2h): Dynamic product card + targeted review.
4. Post-Purchase Repeat
Email 1 (Instant): Order confirmation + delivery expectations.
SMS (+Day 28): Predictive re-order / VIP cross-sell.
